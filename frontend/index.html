Anime 101

What is anime?

Anime is animation associated with Japan and includes theatrical films, television series, online releases, music videos, and direct-to-video productions. It spans many audiences and genres, rather than representing a single genre by itself.

01

Titles and formats

A title may be released as a TV series, movie, OVA, ONA, special, music video, or short-form production.

02

Studios

Animation studios coordinate artistic production. Multiple companies may participate, while AniList identifies primary studios for many titles.

03

Voice actors

Japanese voice actors, often called seiyuu, perform character roles. Their repeated collaborations form professional co-starring networks.

04

Audience signals

AniList provides scores, popularity, favourites, and trending activity. These describe different kinds of audience attention.

TV: broadcast or streamed series MOVIE: theatrical or feature film OVA: original video animation ONA: original net animation Score: weighted user rating out of 100 Popularity: users listing the title

Methodology and limitations

Data source

Data is requested from AniList's GraphQL API. The backend retrieves popular non-adult anime and derives coordinated summaries for the frontend.

Unit of analysis

One record represents one anime entry. Franchise seasons, movies, specials, and related adaptations may appear as separate entries.

Sampling caution

This is a popularity-sorted sample. It is useful for studying highly visible anime, but it is not a random sample or a complete history of the anime industry.

Network definition

A voice-actor link indicates co-starring among Japanese main-role cast records returned by AniList. It does not prove a personal or contractual relationship.

Missing values

Some entries lack scores, dates, studios, episode counts, or cast information. Visualizations exclude missing values only when the corresponding encoding requires them.

Reproducibility

The backend caches processed API responses. The generation time, sample size, and cache status are displayed in the page footer.
