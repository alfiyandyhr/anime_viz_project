What is anime?

Animation associated with Japan — TV series, films, OVA/ONA, and shorts — spanning many audiences and genres rather than one.

TV: broadcast or streamed series MOVIE: theatrical or feature film OVA: original video animation ONA: original net animation Score: weighted user rating out of 100 Popularity: users listing the title Seiyuu: Japanese voice actor
