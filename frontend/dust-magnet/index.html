How these recommendations work

One million comparisons

1,000 popular AniList anime × 1,000 popular TMDB movies. The dust field combines the strongest matches to each movie and shared favorites using the complete score matrix.

Story similarity

Qwen3-Embedding-4B embeds titles, genres, tags, and synopses. Cosine similarity compares the normalized vectors; ratings and popularity do not affect the score.

Reading the magnets

Dots combine strong matches to each movie and the highest average scores across your movies. With multiple magnets, each dot stays closer to its higher-scoring movies, including when scores are zero or negative. Spacing respects this preference order. A single movie gathers a compact cloud through positive-score attraction. Moving magnets changes the layout, never the scores. Distances show each dot’s preference order across movies, not an exact cosine scale or a ranking of different anime. Some nearly collinear arrangements cannot show every preference; a message suggests spreading or resetting the magnets, while the highest-scoring movie stays nearest. Color identifies the highest-scoring movie; pink marks your selection. Click a dot to focus it; click elsewhere or reset to clear. Hover or focus for exact scores. Distances between anime carry no meaning.

A starting point

These are text-based recommendations, not predictions of your enjoyment. Seasons, sequels, and the same animated film in both databases can appear separately. Genre labels are context, not an explanation of the model’s score.
