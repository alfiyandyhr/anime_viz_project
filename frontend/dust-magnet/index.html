About / Data

One million comparisons

1,000 popular AniList anime × 1,000 popular TMDB movies. Anime and movie dust use the same pair scores from the complete score matrix.

Story similarity

Qwen3-Embedding-4B embeds titles, genres, tags, and synopses. Cosine similarity compares the normalized vectors; ratings and popularity do not affect the score.

Genre cohorts

Genre magnets use mean cosine similarity. For each dust title, the score is the arithmetic mean of its signed cosine similarities to every sampled title with that genre in the magnet catalogue. A title can belong to multiple cohorts and contribute to each of their scores. Each magnet contributes one score to the combined average. Filters narrow the dust while the cohorts stay fixed.

Anime studios

Studios group anime by AniList main-studio credit. As in Full Catalogue, Popularity sums list-user counts and Score averages available ratings. A movie magnet averages the studio’s signed cosine similarities to that movie. A Movie genre magnet averages over every scored pair of a studio anime and a movie in the cohort. Each magnet uses all works with data for that magnet. Filters affect which studios appear in the field. The analysis charts cover the catalogue filmographies of the selected studios, or all studios in the field when the selection is empty.

Anime voice actors

Voice actors group anime by AniList Japanese main-role credits. A movie magnet averages the similarities of an actor’s credited anime to that movie. A Movie genre magnet averages across the cohort’s movies. Popularity sums list-user counts, and Score averages available ratings. Score vs. popularity shows anime credited to the selected actors, or all actors in the field when the selection is empty. Shared titles count once.

Anime original creators

Original creators group anime by AniList Original Creator, Original Story, and Original Work credits. Magnets and linked charts work as they do for voice actors. Zoom in for available creator portraits.

Genre and title counts

Anime genre counts distinct credited catalogue anime in the chosen genre for each studio, voice actor, or original creator. Movie genre counts distinct credited catalogue movies in the chosen genre for each actor or writer. Title count counts each group’s full catalogue filmography. Both count magnets use attraction percentiles. Filters change the field while the counts and reference sample stay fixed.

Movie actors

Movie actors group movies by their top five sampled TMDB cast credits. Anime and genre magnets average similarities across each actor’s credited movies. Popularity sums archived TMDB popularity, and Score averages available TMDB ratings. Score vs. popularity rank shows movies credited to the selected actors, or to all actors in the field when the selection is empty. Shared movies count once.

Movie writers

Movie writers group movies by TMDB Writing department credits, including screenplay, story, and source material. The magnets and linked charts follow the same rules as Movie actors. Zoom in for available writer portraits.

Attraction percentiles

Popularity uses AniList users for anime and TMDB popularity for movies. Score uses AniList ratings out of 100 and TMDB ratings out of 10. With a count, Popularity, or Score magnet active, each magnet’s raw values become attraction percentiles from 0 to 1. The reference sample includes dust with values for every active magnet. Ties share the average percentile and a sample of one gets 0.5. Dot count and filters leave the reference sample fixed. The full ranking keeps entries with missing values and supports sorting by each magnet’s raw values.

Reading the magnets

The field samples each magnet’s strongest matches, then fills remaining slots by average cosine or average attraction percentile. Similar scores draw a dot into the space between magnets. Nearby dots can shift it to make room. Hover, focus, or select a dot to see its links. Thicker links show which magnets pull more strongly on that dot. Coloring uses each dot’s highest score across the active magnets. Hover or focus a dot, or use the full ranking table, to compare scores. Select up to three dots with a click, tap, Enter, or Space. Select again to remove a title, or click empty space to remove the latest selection.

Adjusting magnet strength

Each magnet’s strength ranges from 0× to 2×. At 0× the magnet exerts no pull. Higher values draw dots closer when their scores are similar across magnets. Scores and dust selection use equal weights. Reset strengths restores each magnet to 1×.

Reading the charts

Charts use the displayed titles. The full ranking uses the entire dust catalogue. Genres can overlap. With anime dust, AniList popularity counts list users and reception marker size shows favourites. With movie dust, reception, the Movie catalogue, and the Popularity and Score magnets share the archived TMDB snapshot. Movie posters and runtimes come from a separate TMDB details snapshot for those same titles.

A starting point

These are text-based recommendations, not predictions of your enjoyment. Seasons, sequels, and the same animated film in both databases can appear separately. Genre labels are context, not an explanation of the model’s score.
