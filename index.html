What is anime?

Animation associated with Japan — TV series, films, OVA/ONA, and shorts — spanning many audiences and genres rather than one.

TV: broadcast or streamed series MOVIE: theatrical or feature film OVA: original video animation ONA: original net animation Score: weighted user rating out of 100 Popularity: users listing the title Seiyuu: Japanese voice actor

Chapter 03 · Creators

Who makes the hits?

Behind every popular title is a studio and a cast. Some studios pair huge reach with consistently strong scores; others specialize in a genre. And the same Japanese voice actors recur again and again, connecting the characters they play and the works they appear in.

How these recommendations work

One million comparisons

1,000 popular AniList anime × 1,000 popular TMDB movies. The dust view uses the complete score matrix; the connection map shows each movie’s top and bottom matches, with the full ranking available below in pages of 10.

Story similarity

Qwen3-Embedding-4B embeds titles, genres, tags, and synopses. Cosine similarity compares the normalized vectors; ratings and popularity do not affect the score.

Reading the magnets

Dots combine strong matches to each movie and the highest average scores across your movies. Target distance scales with √(2 − 2 × cosine). A two-dimensional layout balances those distances and separates overlapping dots, so positions are approximate. Color identifies the closest movie by score; pink marks your selection. Hover or focus a dot for exact scores. Distances between anime carry no meaning.

A starting point

These are text-based recommendations, not predictions of your enjoyment. Seasons, sequels, and the same animated film in both databases can appear separately. Genre labels are context, not an explanation of the model’s score.

Methodology & limitations

Data source

Data is requested from AniList's GraphQL API. The backend retrieves popular non-adult anime and derives coordinated summaries for the frontend.

Unit of analysis

One record represents one anime entry. Franchise seasons, movies, specials, and related adaptations may appear as separate entries.

Sampling caution

This is a popularity-sorted sample. It is useful for studying highly visible anime, but it is not a random sample or a complete history of the anime industry.

Network definition

The network uses Japanese main-role cast records returned by AniList. Links show who voices a character, which works an actor appears in, and which work contains a character. Character nodes represent roles within individual works. Actors and characters are also linked to peers in the same anime. Up to 35 matching works with the highest popularity are shown to keep the graph readable.

Missing values

Some entries lack scores, dates, studios, episode counts, or cast information. Visualizations exclude missing values only when the corresponding encoding requires them.

Reproducibility

The backend caches processed API responses. The generation time and cache status appear in the left rail.
